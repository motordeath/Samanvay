# ENGINE TOPOLOGY REPORT

## 1. Overview
The Samanvay platform is a distributed operational orchestration system consisting of five primary runtime engines. These engines coordinate human governance, resource allocation, and operational participation boundaries.

## 2. Governance Engine
**Scope:** Core authority, access control, identity, and trust relations.
- **Organizations:** The foundational operational boundary. All needs, events, and memberships scope to an active organization.
- **Memberships:** The linkage between `User` and `Organization`. Acts as the primary human governance state. Lifecycle (`ACTIVE` vs `INACTIVE`) dictates downstream operational propagation.
- **Partnerships:** Cross-organization trust links enabling distributed operations like `Transfers`.
- **Authorization:** `authorization.service.ts` gatekeeps multi-tenant boundaries by inspecting `Membership` and `Organization` active statuses.

**Orchestration Dependencies:** 
- Upstream: None (Root level)
- Downstream: Gates Event creation, Resource Needs/Offers, Volunteer workflows.

## 3. Event Engine
**Scope:** Time-bound operational campaigns.
- **Event Lifecycle:** Dictated by `status` (e.g., `DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED`, `CANCELLED`).
- **Event Authority Propagation:** An event's status cascades downstream. E.g., transitioning an event to `CANCELLED` forces `OPEN` Volunteer Needs to transition to `CLOSED`.

**Orchestration Dependencies:**
- Upstream: Governance Engine (Organization)
- Downstream: Resource Needs, Volunteer Needs.

## 4. Resource Engine
**Scope:** Material inventory coordination, supply/demand mapping.
- **Resource Needs:** The material demand generated by an organization or event.
- **Resource Lots & Offers:** The material supply and corresponding commitments mapped to needs.
- **Transfers:** The movement logistics between organizations, protected by `Partnership` requirements.

**Orchestration Dependencies:**
- Upstream: Governance (Partnerships for transfers), Event Engine (optional scoping).
- Downstream: Logistics / Audit Engine.

## 5. Volunteer Engine
**Scope:** Human operational participation and staffing.
- **Volunteers:** Global profiles tracking skills and availability.
- **Staffing (VolunteerNeeds):** The operational demand for human effort.
- **Assignments:** The lifecycle-bound relationship (`ASSIGNED` -> `CHECKED_IN` -> `CHECKED_OUT` -> `COMPLETED`, or `CANCELLED`) representing a fulfilled need.
- **Attendance:** Cryptographic/Verifiable proof of participation tied to a non-cancelled assignment.

**Orchestration Dependencies:**
- Upstream: Governance Engine (Membership status dictates Assignment cancellation), Event Engine (Event cancellation dictates Need closure).

## 6. Audit Engine
**Scope:** Immutable historical traceability and operational truth.
- **Audit Logs (Core):** Traces organization, event, resource, and membership operations.
- **Volunteer Audits:** Segregated trace logs for operational participation workflows (e.g., assignment transitions, cancellations).

**Orchestration Dependencies:**
- Upstream: Fed asynchronously or transactionally by ALL other engines. MUST share transactional boundaries with core propagations to guarantee truthfulness.
